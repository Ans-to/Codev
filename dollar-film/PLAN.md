# The dollar's global role — 30-second film

A short widescreen, text-led 3D explainer. Dark navy, warm gold and ivory. A rotating globe, a gold dollar coin, orbital paths, and restrained, original instrumental music carry the story. Designed to be understandable without sound.

| Time | Beat | Visual | Text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | Hook | Dollar coin arrives in front of a turning globe | Why the dollar runs the world |
| 5–10 s | History | 1944, coin and globe | Bretton Woods tied currencies to the dollar; the dollar was linked to gold |
| 10–15 s | Trade | Trade routes orbit the globe | Oil and global trade are often priced and settled in dollars |
| 15–20 s | Reserves | Gold bars rise | Deep U.S. Treasury markets offer central banks a place to hold reserves |
| 20–25 s | Network | Routes and coin | Banks and businesses use dollars because so many others do |
| 25–30 s | Context | A closing view of the globe | The dollar leads today, but its role can change |

One 38° camera lens moves on a Bezier path. Motion is deterministic in time. The soundtrack is synthesized for this film; no third-party imagery or music is used.
